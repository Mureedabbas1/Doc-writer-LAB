Practical guide

Convert Text Case Online: complete guide

A text case converter changes capitalisation without retyping a paragraph. It helps standardise headings, labels, titles and copied text.

When should you use this tool?

Uppercase suits short labels, while sentence case is often easier to read in longer passages. Follow the style guide for formal titles.

How to use it

Paste or type text, choose the desired case, review the result and copy it into your document. Check names, acronyms and specialised terms.

Tips for better results

Automatic conversion cannot recognise every proper noun or brand style. Proofread names and abbreviations.

Frequently asked questions

Will it rewrite my text? No, it changes letter capitalisation. Should I proofread? Yes, especially names, acronyms and titles.
