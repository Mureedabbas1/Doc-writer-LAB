Practical guide

Add Page Numbers to PDF: complete guide

Adding page numbers makes long PDFs easier to read, reference and print. It is especially useful for reports, coursework, proposals and scanned document packs.

When should you use this tool?

Use page numbering when readers need to cite a page or follow a document in order. Before applying numbers, check whether the cover page should be numbered and whether the numbering should begin at 1 or a later page.

How to add page numbers to PDF

Open the tool and choose your PDF. Review the numbering options available, apply the numbers, then download and inspect several pages—including the first and last—to confirm placement and sequence.

Tips for better results

Check that numbers do not cover footnotes, signatures or existing page content. Keep the original PDF until you have verified the numbered copy.
