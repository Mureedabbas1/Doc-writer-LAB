Privacy and important limitations

The tool is designed to process selected files in your browser rather than sending them to a PDF upload server. Keep your original file until you have checked the result.

This tool places a typed name as visible text. It is not a cryptographic digital signature, identity verification, or a guarantee that a recipient will accept it for legal purposes.

Sign a PDF: complete guide

Adding a signature to a PDF can prepare a document for routine review or exchange without printing and scanning it.

When should you use this tool?

A drawn or placed signature can suit everyday workflows, but regulated or high-value transactions may require a specific e-signature service or certificate-based digital signature.

How to use it

Select the PDF, add your signature using the available method, position it and download the result. Reopen the file to check visibility and placement.

Tips for better results

A visible signature image is not necessarily a cryptographic digital signature and may not provide identity verification or tamper evidence.
