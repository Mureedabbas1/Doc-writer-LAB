ABOUT DOC WRITER LAB

Simple tools for everyday documents

Doc Writer Lab is a browser-based website offering free PDF and document utilities. Our goal is to make common document tasks—such as merging, splitting, compressing, converting and signing PDFs—straightforward to access without requiring an account.

How the tools work

Supported tools are designed to process selected files in your browser on your device. Available features and limits may depend on your browser, device and file. Always review the result and keep a copy of important originals.

What we provide

  • Online PDF and document tools
  • Guides that explain common document tasks
  • A simple website that can be used on supported phones and computers

Questions or feedback

For bug reports and questions, visit our public GitHub issue tracker. Please do not post private documents, passwords or sensitive personal information there.
