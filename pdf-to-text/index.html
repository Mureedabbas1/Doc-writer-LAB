Practical guide

Extract Text from PDF: complete guide

Extracting text from a PDF helps reuse paragraphs, quotations or data without manually retyping the document.

When should you use this tool?

Text extraction works best on digitally generated PDFs. Scanned pages may require OCR, and complex columns or tables can affect reading order.

How to use it

Select the PDF, start extraction and review the output. Compare headings, line breaks, punctuation and numbers against the original pages.

Tips for better results

Verify important figures and quotations. Scanned PDFs may return little text unless OCR is available.

Frequently asked questions

Why is no text extracted? The PDF may be scanned or use unsupported encoding. Is extracted text always exact? No, proofread it.
