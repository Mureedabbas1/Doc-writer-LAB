Privacy Policy

How Doc Writer Lab handles information when you use our online document tools.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Doc Writer Lab provides online tools for working with PDFs and documents. This policy describes the main ways information may be handled when you visit the site or use a tool.

1. Information you enter

The information you provide depends on the tool and task. Supported tools are designed to process files in your browser where practical, but features may differ and some operations may rely on external libraries or services. Check the instructions shown for the particular tool before submitting information. Avoid using confidential or sensitive documents unless you have verified that the tool meets your needs.

2. File processing

When a tool processes a file in your browser, the file is handled by code running on your device. Third-party scripts or libraries may be loaded to enable a feature, and this policy does not claim that every tool or dependency has identical data practices. If a feature sends information to an external service, that service's terms and privacy policy may apply.

3. Technical data and analytics

The hosting platform and service providers may process technical information required to deliver, secure and maintain the website, such as request, browser, device and error information. Doc Writer Lab uses Google Analytics to understand website usage and improve its pages. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies.

4. Advertising

Doc Writer Lab may show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise ads depending on your settings and applicable policies. See Google's advertising policy or visit My Ad Center to review available choices.

5. Third-party services

Tools may use third-party libraries, APIs or linked websites. Those providers may receive information needed to deliver the selected function and handle it according to their own policies. Doc Writer Lab does not control third-party services.

6. Retention and security

Files handled locally may remain in browser memory or on your device until the session ends or you remove them. Information processed by an external provider is subject to that provider's retention practices. Reasonable precautions are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

This is a general-purpose online tools website and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

You can stop using a tool, avoid submitting sensitive information, manage browser cookies and review Google's privacy and advertising settings. Available legal rights depend on your location and applicable law.

9. Policy updates

This policy may be revised when the website or its tools change. The date above shows the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Doc Writer Lab contact page.
