Practical guide

Word Counter: complete guide

A word counter measures text length so writers can check assignments, articles, applications, captions and content limits.

When should you use this tool?

Character counts help when platforms impose strict limits. Different services may count hyphens, apostrophes, numbers and symbols differently.

How to use it

Paste or type the text into the counter and review the word and character totals. Recheck after your final edits if a submission has a strict limit.

Tips for better results

Do not rely on word count alone to judge clarity. Follow the destination’s rules for headings, footnotes and references.

Frequently asked questions

Does word count equal character count? No, characters include letters and punctuation and may include spaces. Why can counts differ? Apps can use different counting rules.
