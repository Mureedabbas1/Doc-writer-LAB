How to Remove Pages from a PDF

Removing unneeded pages can make a document shorter and easier to send, but always confirm the page numbers before saving.

Explore free tools →

Identify the pages

Open the PDF and note the actual page positions. Printed page numbers may not match viewer positions because of covers, contents pages or unnumbered inserts.

Select pages to remove

Open Remove Pages, choose the PDF and select the page numbers the tool asks for. Review the selection before processing; removing the wrong page can be hard to notice in a long document.

Save a new copy

Download the edited PDF under a new name and keep the original until you have checked the output. Confirm the first and last pages, total page count, and any important attachments or signature pages.

Do not confuse removal with redaction

Deleting a page removes that page from the output, but it is not the same as securely redacting sensitive text within a retained page. Use a proper redaction workflow for confidential information and verify that hidden content is actually removed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove multiple pages at once?

Yes, use the page-selection controls supported by the tool.

Does page removal affect the source file?

The tool creates a separate output; keep your original backup.
