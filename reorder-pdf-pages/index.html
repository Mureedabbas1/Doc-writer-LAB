Practical guide

Reorder PDF Pages: complete guide

Reordering pages helps correct a scan sequence or arrange sections clearly without recreating the document.

When should you use this tool?

Use this when a scan is out of sequence or attachments should follow a report. Plan the intended order first to avoid missing or duplicated pages.

How to use it

Open the PDF, move pages into the intended order using available controls, export and review the document from start to finish.

Tips for better results

Check printed page labels, chapters and appendices—not just thumbnails. Keep the original to make recovery easier.

Frequently asked questions

Will printed page numbers change? Reordering usually changes sequence, not numbers printed on the pages. Review the final order before sending.
