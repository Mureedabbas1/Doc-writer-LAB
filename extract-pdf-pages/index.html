Extract Pages from PDF: complete guide

Extracting pages creates a smaller PDF containing only the pages you need, such as selected chapters, invoices or supporting evidence.

When should you use this tool?

Extraction is useful when sharing a subset of a larger document. It differs from splitting a PDF into every individual page because it focuses on a chosen subset.

How to use it

Select your PDF, specify pages or a range using the interface, export the new PDF and check its first and last pages.

Tips for better results

Keep pages in the intended order and ensure no required attachment or disclaimer was omitted. Retain the full original.
