Percentage Calculator: complete guide

A percentage calculator solves common questions, such as finding a percentage of a number or comparing a part with a whole.

When should you use this tool?

Percentages are used for discounts, tax estimates, scores and increases or decreases. Understand which value is the base before interpreting a result.

How to use it

Enter the required values, choose the calculation and review the answer. For financial or official use, confirm inputs and applicable rounding rules.

Tips for better results

A result is only as accurate as its inputs. Taxes, fees and discounts can have special rules beyond simple percentage arithmetic.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find 20% of a number? Multiply by 0.20. Why does the base matter? Percentage changes depend on which original amount is used.
