How to Turn JPG Images into a PDF

Turning photos into a PDF is useful for combining receipts, forms, notes or scanned pages into one file.

Explore free tools →

Prepare the images

Choose clear, correctly oriented images. Crop unnecessary background and make sure text is readable before conversion. Rename files or arrange them in the intended order if the tool follows selection order.

Create the PDF

Open JPG to PDF, select your images and start the conversion. Check the resulting page sequence and whether the tool fits each image to a page or uses a fixed page size.

Check readability and size

Open the output on a phone and desktop if possible. Inspect small print, rotation and margins. Large photographs can make a PDF sizeable; compression may help, but rasterising pages again can reduce clarity.

Keep records safely

For receipts, identity documents or signed forms, store the output in a private location and share it only with the intended recipient. Delete temporary downloads from shared computers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine several JPG files?

Yes, the tool is intended to combine multiple JPG images into one PDF.

Will the PDF text be searchable?

Photos generally remain images inside the PDF; OCR is needed to recognise text in scans.
